Add an email signature in Gmail

  1. Click Copy email signature on the card you want.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → General → Signature and click Create new.
  3. Click inside the signature box and press Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on Mac).
  4. Scroll down and click Save Changes. You can also paste it straight into a new email.

Printing the hard copies

  • Send the PDF to the printer as it is. Page 1 is the front and page 2 is the back.
  • Each page has 3 mm bleed and a marked cut size, so colours run to the edge after trimming.
  • Rohana Hettiarachchi & Company and AIA print at 90 × 55 mm. Sunbird prints at 90 × 50 mm; its map side is upright, so the printer turns that page.
  • Ask for a printed proof first: screen colours can look slightly different on paper.
